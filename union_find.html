Algorithm Theoretical Bounds

WQUPC (Nearly Linear): With tree weighting and path compression, any sequence of $M$ operations on $N$ elements takes $O(N + M \alpha(N))$ time, where $\alpha(N) \le 4$ for all practical universe sizes!

Quick-Find: Fast $O(1)$ find, but expensive $O(N)$ union. Takes quadratic $O(M \cdot N)$ time for $M$ unions.

Quick-Union: Fast union if trees are flat, but trees can degrade into linear chains of depth $N$, leading to $O(M \cdot N)$ worst-case.
