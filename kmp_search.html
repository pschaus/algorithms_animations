KMP Theoretical Guarantee

No Backup in Text: Text pointer $i$ never decreases ($i++$). Ideal for streaming input where buffering is prohibitive.

Linear Time: Search executes at most $N$ character examinations. Constructing the DFA takes $O(M \cdot R)$ time and space.
