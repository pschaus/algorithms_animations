Heap Mathematical Invariants

Max-Heap Property: Every node's key is $\ge$ both of its children keys ($pq[k] \ge pq[2k]$ and $pq[k] \ge pq[2k+1]$).

Logarithmic Operations: Height is bounded by $\lfloor \lg n \rfloor$. Both insert() and delMax() require at most $O(\log n)$ comparisons and swaps.
