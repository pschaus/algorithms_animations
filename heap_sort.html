Educational Invariants

Heap Invariant: Key at node $k$ is greater than or equal to keys at children $2k$ and $2k+1$.

O(N) Construction: Sinking from $N/2$ down to 1 takes linear time because most nodes are near the leaves with tiny sink heights: $\sum_{h=0}^{\lg N} \frac{N}{2^{h+1}} h \le N$.

In-Place: Only $O(1)$ auxiliary memory required! No secondary array allocated.
